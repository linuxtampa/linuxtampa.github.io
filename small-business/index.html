For Small Business Owners

If you run a business in Tampa or Lakeland, FL (or want remote help), this page is for you. I mostly work with enterprise clients—but I also help smaller shops cut SaaS waste, keep data on gear they control, and ship websites and Linux servers without handing everything to BigTech.

What I can help with

Cloud & SaaS costs

Audit the stack, find unused seats and overbuilt cloud, and right-size—or move workloads off the expensive plan entirely.

  • Cut monthly subscriptions you barely use
  • Move suitable workloads to a simple server instead of cloud markup
  • Clear recommendations: keep, shrink, or replace

Your data, on your gear

Move files, passwords, and camera footage off someone else’s SaaS and onto hardware you own—in your building, under your control.

  • Nextcloud — your own Drive/Dropbox on your server
  • Bitwarden — team password management you host
  • Security cameras & NVR — own the footage, no monthly cloud DVR fee

Private AI

Use AI where it helps day-to-day work—without pasting customer data, contracts, and internal docs into someone else’s model.

  • Practical AI in your existing workflow
  • Local / private AI on your own hardware
  • AI features on a site or app you already run

Websites

Design, build, and improve sites that are fast, accessible, and easy to maintain—from a fresh build to incremental fixes on what you already have, with coding agents in the loop for scaffolding, refactors, and review when they earn their keep.

  • GoTonight.com — live music event search engine covering multiple Florida markets; sole developer and operator for 15+ years (ColdFusion/Java, MSSQL, jQuery; fuzzy event/artist dedup; layered anti-abuse and Google Maps geocoding)
  • WordPress site for professional musician Justin Nurin

Linux servers

Stand up or take over a Linux box—on-site or on DigitalOcean or any other hosting provider or CDN—and keep it running.

  • Install and maintain self-hosted apps
  • Automated backups
  • Ongoing remote admin and monitoring when something breaks

What I don’t do

  • Windows PCs or Windows Server
  • Microsoft 365 (Outlook, SharePoint, OneDrive)
  • Website content updates or copywriting
  • SEO

How it works

Project or hourly (time & materials)—no long-term contract required. You pay for what you need.

Tampa and Lakeland clients can get on-site visits; most ongoing support is remote.
