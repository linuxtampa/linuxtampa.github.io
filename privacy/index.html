Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

LinuxTampa, LLC ("we," "us") operates linuxtampa.com. This policy describes what information our website collects and how we use it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are read, where traffic comes from, and general location (country or city level). This helps us improve content and focus on what is useful to prospective clients.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. Google may process data on servers in the United States and other countries. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we measure

  • Page views and navigation paths
  • Referring sites and search terms (when available)
  • Approximate geography (country, region, city)
  • Device type, browser, and screen size
  • Outbound link clicks (for example Calendly, LinkedIn, GitHub)
  • File downloads (for example resume PDFs)
  • Scroll depth on long pages (for example blog posts)

We do not use analytics to collect passwords, payment card numbers, or the contents of email you send us outside this website.

Demographics

If enough aggregated traffic is available, GA4 may report age, gender, and interest categories. We enable this only through Google's optional Google signals settings. We do not receive individual income or salary data from analytics.

Your choices

You can limit analytics collection by:

  • Using a browser extension or setting that blocks analytics scripts
  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Adjusting cookie preferences in your browser
